About this product

Pigeon peas (Cajanus cajan), called gandules or guandú in Spanish, are a legume grown in Peru mainly on the northern coast, in Piura and Lambayeque. The plant is a shrub native to India that thrives in warm, dry climates. The peas are harvested green, while the grain is tender, and packed in salted water to keep their color and their characteristic, slightly sweet and herbal flavor. Like other pulses, they provide plant protein and fiber.

Culinary uses

  • Arroz con gandules (Puerto Rico)
  • Sancocho and pigeon pea soup
  • Salads
  • Stews with pork or chicken
